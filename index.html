The Crisis Facing Our Oceans in 2025

Coral Reef Decline

The 2023–2025 global bleaching event was the worst on record, affecting 84.4% of the world's coral reefs. Scientists warn that without big changes, up to 90% could be gone by 2050.

Why Corals Are Dying

Warming oceans and acidification stress corals, causing them to expel their colourful symbiotic algae. They turn white and often die if conditions don't improve quickly.

Overfishing

35.5% of global fish stocks are currently overfished. This disrupts marine food webs and makes coral reefs more vulnerable to collapse.

Microplastics Pollution

Tiny plastic particles are found in every ocean and in 100% of Great Lakes fish tested near Toronto. They harm marine life and move up the food chain.

Toronto's Connection to the Global Oceans

The Don River carries hundreds of billions of microplastic particles into Lake Ontario each year. From there, water flows into the St. Lawrence River and the Atlantic. Our everyday choices here in the GTA affect the entire ocean ecosystem.
